How Target Grade Works
What does it do?

Target Grade tells you what score you need on a remaining assessment to reach your desired final grade in a subject.

Understanding Assessment Weights

Each grading component (Quizzes, Midterm, Final Exam, etc.) has a weight — how much of your final grade it counts for.

Quizzes20% weight
Midterm Exam30% weight
Final Exam50% weight

If you score 90% on Quizzes (worth 20%), quizzes contribute 90 × 20% = 18 points to your final grade.

What you enter vs. what the system uses
Desired Final Grade — the overall subject grade you want to achieve (e.g., 90%)
Required Score — the score you need on that specific assessment (e.g., get 85% on the Final Exam)
Weight is not editable here — the component weights come from your grading setup and are not changed by this calculator
Simple example
Quiz score so far80% × 20% = 16 pts
Projects score so far88% × 30% = 26.4 pts
Current total42.4 pts
Desired final grade90%
Final Exam (50% weight) — need:(90 − 42.4) ÷ 50% = 95.2%

So you need to score 95.2% on the Final Exam to reach a final grade of 90%.
